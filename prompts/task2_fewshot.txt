You are analyzing sentiment in movie reviews from the IMDB
dataset. These are informal, user-written reviews of films.
Classify each review into one of two sentiments:

Positive: The reviewer recommends the film or evaluates it
favorably overall — praises acting, writing, direction,
atmosphere, or emotional impact, or reports enjoying it
(e.g., "the pacing drags a bit, but the ending floored me",
"an underrated gem I'll rewatch").

Negative: The reviewer dismisses the film or evaluates it
unfavorably overall — criticizes acting, writing, pacing,
or coherence, reports boredom or disappointment, or warns
others away (e.g., "two hours I'll never get back", "great
premise, wasted on a lazy script").

Judge the reviewer's overall verdict on the film, not the
emotional tone of the plot they describe: a review of a
tragic or violent film can be Positive, and a cheerful
film can be reviewed Negatively. If the review is mixed,
decide which way the reviewer's final recommendation leans.
Sarcasm and irony indicate the opposite of the literal
wording.

is one of "positive"/"negative", "confidence" is a number from
0.0 to 1.0, and "evidence" is one concise sentence stating what
in the review supports the label, stated directly rather than
reasoned out.

Output format:
{"label": "<positive|negative>", "confidence": <0.0-1.0>, "evidence": "<one sentence>"}
